# Utah — rules to confirm

Read **2026-11-08**. Used by the app's “Check the rules” screen (`app/js/law.js`). State rules only: your county and city may add their own (sign rules, contribution limits, filing details). **Not legal advice — confirm with your election office.** Utah's elections office: [vote.utah.gov](https://vote.utah.gov/).

## Running
- **Filing (city and town offices):** declaration of candidacy with the city recorder in early June of odd-numbered years; the recorder posts the exact days. County and state offices file with the county clerk or Lieutenant Governor on other dates. — [Utah Code 10-3-301](https://le.utah.gov/xcode/Title10/Chapter3/10-3-S301.html)

## Money (city and town candidates)
- Reports due 7 days before the primary; 28 and 7 days before the general; 30 days after the general (and 30 days after the primary if eliminated). Itemize everything unless both contributions and spending stay at $500 or less. Reports are posted online. Late reports are fined; filing the last pre-election report more than a day late can remove the candidate from the ballot.
- Anonymous contributions over $50 can't be kept; give the excess away within 30 days.
- Separate campaign account; no personal use.
- Source: [Utah Code 10-3-208](https://le.utah.gov/xcode/Title10/Chapter3/10-3-S208.html)
- **Contribution limits:** none in state law for state candidates; cities and counties may set their own. — [Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Campaign_finance_requirements_in_Utah)

## Materials
- **“Paid for by”:** campaign-paid ads say so; if someone else pays with the candidate's OK, name the payer and that it was authorized. From **January 1, 2027** the line must “clearly and conspicuously” identify the payer. — [Utah Code 20A-11-901](https://le.utah.gov/xcode/Title20A/Chapter11/20A-11-S901.html), [H.B. 33 (2026)](https://le.utah.gov/~2026/bills/static/HB0033.html)
- **Exempt items:** through 2026, lawn signs 4 × 8 ft or smaller, bumper stickers, buttons, pens and small items. From 2027, signs smaller than 24 × 18 in, and candidate-paid signs that clearly name the candidate. The kit's 24 × 18 in yard sign is exactly at the line — keep the disclaimer on it.
- **Email and texts:** the rule covers “general public political advertising”; email and texts aren't named. Ask.

## Yard signs
- From 2026: no signs on utility poles, light poles, traffic signals or railroad signals (an infraction). Adjacent owners may place signs in the park strip. Local sign rules must be content-neutral. Signs on a road right-of-way must come down within 14 days after the election. — [H.B. 33 (2026)](https://le.utah.gov/~2026/bills/static/HB0033.html)
- A city can't prohibit political signs more than 150 ft from a polling place. — [Utah Code 20A-3a-501](https://le.utah.gov/xcode/Title20A/Chapter3A/20A-3a-S501.html)

## Voting
- Active voters are mailed a ballot about 3 weeks before Election Day (21 days in 2026); from 2029 voters must opt in. Ballots must be **received** by 8 p.m. on Election Day (a postmark no longer counts). From 2026 the return envelope asks for the last four digits of the voter's ID or Social Security number. — [H.B. 300 (2025)](https://le.utah.gov/~2025/bills/static/HB0300.html)
- Online and mail registration closes 11 days before Election Day. Early voting dates are set by each county (Cache County 2026: Oct 26–30). The app's “Use these dates on my calendar” sets ballots out at −21 days and registration at −11.
- **Electioneering:** none inside a polling place or in public areas within 150 ft of the building; a ballot drop box counts as a polling place. Class A misdemeanor. Carrying someone else's voted ballot is limited to household members, helpers for voters who need it, and election and postal workers. — [Utah Code 20A-3a-501](https://le.utah.gov/xcode/Title20A/Chapter3A/20A-3a-S501.html)

## Voter lists
- Statewide voter list: $1,050 from the Lieutenant Governor, no updates or refunds. County lists from the county clerk. Birth dates withheld (age range only); protected (“at-risk”) voters left out. “Who has voted” and “who was sent a ballot” reports: $35 per election. Ask what uses are allowed before uploading the list to an ad platform. — [vote.utah.gov](https://vote.utah.gov/obtain-voter-registration-or-election-data/)
