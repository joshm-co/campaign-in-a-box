# Worked Example: Dana Reyes, Rivertown City Council, District 4

*Fictional candidate, fictional city. Use it to see what a finished message box looks like.*

**Race:** Nonpartisan, two candidates, plurality wins.
**Top issues from the interview:** (1) flooding on the east side, (2) slow permits for small businesses, (3) a safe walking route to Lincoln Elementary.

|  | **About Dana** | **About the incumbent** |
|---|---|---|
| **What Dana says** | **Q1.** Civil engineer, 15 years in District 4. Led the neighborhood effort that got the Mill Creek culvert cleared after the 2023 flood. Fixes things, then explains how. | **Q2.** Eight years on council; the east-side drainage study has been "pending" for six of them (council minutes). Voted twice to delay the permit-streamlining pilot. |
| **What the incumbent says** | **Q3.** "No government experience." "Will raise fees to pay for drainage." Missed two municipal elections (public voting record). | **Q4.** Experience and relationships; "kept taxes flat"; knows how City Hall works. |

**Q3 responses:**
- *No government experience:* "I've spent 15 years building projects on time and on budget. That's the experience the drainage project needs."
- *Fees:* "I'll pursue the state and federal flood grants we've left on the table first. No new fee without a public vote of council and a cost breakdown residents can read."
- *Missed elections:* "I missed two local elections when my kids were small. I should have voted. It's part of why I'm running — local decisions matter and I didn't treat them that way."

**Core message:**

> Dana Reyes will finally fix east-side flooding, because she's an engineer who already got Mill Creek cleared — while City Hall has left it "pending" for six years.

(23 words. Q1 proof + Q2 contrast + local issue.)
