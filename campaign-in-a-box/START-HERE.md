# Start Here — A First-Time Candidate's One Page

You don't need campaign experience to use this kit. You need a few hours, a phone or laptop, and the willingness to look at numbers honestly. Do these in order.

> **Easiest:** open the app at **https://joshm-co.github.io/campaign-in-a-box/** and follow it — it walks you through every step below, one question per screen, and makes your materials. Your answers stay on your device. The files listed below are the same method, for people who prefer a spreadsheet.

## Week 1: Gather (about 4–6 hours)

1. **Confirm you can run.** Call your county or state election office. Ask: filing deadline, filing fee or petition signatures, residency requirement, and who you file campaign finance reports with. ⚖️ STATE LAW VARIES
2. **Collect your inputs.** Work through `inputs/intake-checklist.md`. The election office and the Census Bureau's free data cover most of it.
3. **Do the candidate interview.** Have a friend ask you the questions in `inputs/candidate-interview.md` and write down your answers *as you said them*. Your natural words are better than polished ones.

## Week 2: Decide (about 4–6 hours)

4. **Find your win number.** Open `strategy/strategy-workbook.xlsx` → `Win Number` tab. This is how many votes you need. Every other decision serves this number.
5. **Pick your precincts.** Paste past results into the `Precincts` tab. The sheet sorts every precinct into **Persuade**, **Turn Out**, or **Skip**. Read `strategy/precinct-targeting.md` for why.
6. **Fill in your message box.** Use `strategy/message-box.md`. End with one sentence that says why you're running. You'll repeat it a thousand times.
7. **Research yourself.** Go through `strategy/vulnerability-checklist.md` before an opponent or reporter does. Fix what you can; prepare an honest answer for the rest.
8. **Build your calendar and budget.** `Calendar` and `Budget` tabs. Enter Election Day and your budget range; the dates and dollar splits fill in.

## Weeks 3–4: Make your materials (Step 2, about 6–10 hours)

9. **Fill in one file.** Copy `creative/campaign.example.json` to `creative/campaign.json` and replace the fictional content with your own: core message, message box (with a source for every contrast point), three issues, dates, links and your "Paid for by" wording.
10. **Write 5–10 slogans** using `creative/slogans.md`. Label each with its message-box quadrant.
11. **Pick colors and fonts** from `creative/brand/` — or ask a volunteer to run the build for you. One command makes your yard sign, door hangers, palm cards, mailers, flier, social graphics, ads, emails and logo.
12. **Put up your website** (`creative/website/`) and connect the volunteer form and donate button.
13. **Start political-ad verification now** if you'll run Facebook or Google ads. It can take weeks.
14. **Check before you print.** Real photo in, disclaimer confirmed ⚖️, dates confirmed ⚖️, QR codes scanned, translations reviewed. The full checklist is in `creative/print/README.md`.

## Then

Take everything into **Step 3 (Validation)**: test your slogans with real voters before you print in volume.

## Three rules that matter more than anything in this kit

- **Talk to voters at their doors.** For local races, nothing else comes close.
- **Ask for money and help directly.** Most people who give were asked by name.
- **Check the law in your state.** Look for ⚖️ throughout the kit, and see `STATE-LAW-FLAGS.md`. When in doubt, ask the election office — they answer candidate questions for free.
