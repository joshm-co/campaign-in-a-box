# Yard sign legibility at 35 mph

Rule of thumb used: about **30 feet of readable distance per inch of capital-letter height** for bold sans type in high contrast (common sign-industry guidance; real results depend on light, angle and eyesight). At 35 mph a car covers about 51 feet per second.

| Piece | Element | Text | Cap height | Readable to | Seconds at 35 mph |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| yard-sign | First name | CHRIS | 1.8 in | ~53 ft | ~1.0 s |
| yard-sign | Last name | PORTER | 3.9 in | ~117 ft | ~2.3 s |
| yard-sign | Office line | CITY COUNCIL · DISTRICT 4 | 1.1 in | ~34 ft | ~0.7 s |
| yard-sign | Slogan | Fix the flooding. Finally. | 1.2 in | ~36 ft | ~0.7 s |
| yard-sign | Web address | chrisporter.example | 0.4 in | ~12 ft | ~0.2 s |
| yard-sign | Primary read (name + office) | 6 words | | | |

Targets: name ≥ 3.5 in caps, 7 or fewer words in the primary read, contrast ≥ 7:1, no thin or script fonts, no photos on yard signs.
