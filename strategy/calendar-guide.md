# Campaign Calendar — Worked Backward from Election Day

Start at Election Day and work backward. With mail and early voting, many voters cast ballots weeks before Election Day — so "Election Day" is really an election *period*, and your persuasion has to land before ballots arrive.

The workbook's `Calendar` tab calculates every date below from Election Day. **Replace the default offsets with your actual local dates** — every row marked ⚖️ is set by state or county law.

## Key dates (defaults are placeholders, not law)

| Milestone | Default offset | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Election Day | 0 | |
| Final GOTV push (doors, calls, texts to supporters) | last 4 days | Volunteers focus only on supporters who haven't voted |
| Early in-person voting begins | ⚖️ varies widely; placeholder −14 days | Some states have no early in-person voting |
| Mail ballots sent to voters | ⚖️ placeholder −30 days | Federal law requires ballots for military and overseas voters to go out 45 days before federal elections; domestic mail ballot timing is set by your state |
| Voter registration deadline | ⚖️ placeholder −29 days | Some states allow same-day registration |
| Last campaign finance report before the election | ⚖️ check state calendar | Often due 1–2 weeks out |
| Candidate filing deadline | ⚖️ often months before | Missing it ends the campaign |

## Production lead times (work backward from when it must arrive)

Mail and print take longer than first-time candidates expect. The workbook back-calculates "start by" dates.

| Item | Design & approval | Printing | Delivery | Total lead time |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Mailer (USPS Marketing Mail) | 10–14 days | 5–7 days | 3–10 days | ~4–5 weeks |
| Mailer (First-Class Mail) | 10–14 days | 5–7 days | 1–5 days | ~3–4 weeks |
| Yard signs | 7 days | 7–14 days | — | ~3 weeks |
| Door hangers / palm cards / fliers | 7 days | 5–10 days | — | ~2–3 weeks |
| Digital ads | 5–7 days | Platform review and political-ad verification can take days or weeks | — | ~2 weeks; **start verification early** |

Ask your printer and mail house for their real timelines and build in a buffer. Printers get swamped close to elections.

## Timing mail to ballots

Your most important persuasion mail should land **right before or as mail ballots arrive in homes**, not on the weekend before Election Day. A common pattern:

1. **Introduction mailer** — about 1 week before ballots go out
2. **Contrast / issue mailer** — the week ballots arrive
3. **Closing / GOTV reminder** — about 7–10 days before Election Day, to supporters

## Phases

| Phase | When | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| Foundation | Filing → ~12 weeks out | Raise early money, recruit volunteers, finish strategy and creative |
| Persuasion | ~12 weeks → ballots out | Doors in Persuade precincts, identify supporters, earned media |
| Ballot period | Ballots out → Election Day | Mail lands, chase ballots from supporters who haven't returned them |
| GOTV | Final 4 days | Only supporters who haven't voted yet |
| Wrap-up | After Election Day | Thank-yous, sign removal deadline ⚖️, final finance report ⚖️ |
